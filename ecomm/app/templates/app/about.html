Our Origin

Re-engineering The Dairy Supply Chain

MilkMart was incorporated with its registered office in ABC City. Recognizing that supermarket milk takes 4 to 5 days to reach consumers after extensive chemical processing, we built a radical farm-direct cold chain model.

Through our unique "Relationship Farming" initiative, we partner directly with over 1,200 local family-owned dairy farms. We supply vetted organic cattle feed, provide veterinary healthcare, and pay guaranteed premium rates well above wholesale cartels.
